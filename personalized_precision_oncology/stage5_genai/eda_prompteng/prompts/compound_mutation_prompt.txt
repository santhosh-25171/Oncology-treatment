MANDATORY RESPONSE STRUCTURE:

The evaluation response must strictly enforce the Three-Tier Evidence Separation:

1. [SUPPORTED EVIDENCE]
   - Detail the known single-agent oncogenic potency and signaling cascades for each driver alteration in isolation.
   - Describe standard-of-care monotherapy benchmarks for each single alteration according to current clinical guidelines.
   - Document any known biochemical crosstalk (e.g., MAPK-ERK vs PI3K-AKT pathway redundancy).

2. [SYNTHETIC ASSUMPTION]
   - Detail the modeled co-occurrence assumption (e.g., dual clonal coexistence in the same tumor cell versus distinct subclonal populations).
   - Specify modeled variant allele fractions (VAFs) or copy number amplifications illustrating clonal dominance or subclonal divergence.
   - State clearly that multi-driver concurrence represents an extreme stress-test condition to probe agent hierarchy selection.

3. [UNKNOWN / INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE]
   - Acknowledge that the therapeutic hierarchy for concurrent major oncogenic drivers is clinically undefined due to rarity and exclusion from standard clinical trials.
   - State whether the co-occurrence pattern is "not_observed_in_reference_baseline" or observed with sparse representation.
   - Outline the primary mechanistic dilemmas (e.g., risk of concurrent toxicity with dual full-dose TKIs vs selective subclonal pressure with sequential monotherapy).
